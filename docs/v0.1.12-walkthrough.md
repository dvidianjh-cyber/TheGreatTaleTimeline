# v0.1.12 Walkthrough: Measure Tool World Anchoring

## Overview
This update completes the Measure Tool overhaul by fundamentally changing how the engine tracks and renders measurements. Instead of drawing static lines on your screen, the engine now mathematically anchors your measurements to the actual timeline data (Time Units).

## What's Changed
1. **World Mapping:**
   - When you click to draw a measurement, `MeasureTool.js` now internally stores the `startTu` and `endTu` (Time Units) of your line, rather than its raw pixel coordinates.
2. **Dynamic Projection:**
   - The tool now subscribes to the engine's central `RENDER_DIRTY` event payload.
   - Whenever you zoom, pan, or resize the canvas, the tool automatically re-calculates the correct screen pixel positions based on the current zoom scale (`state.pixelsPerTU`) and your horizontal scroll offset (`state.panOffset.x`).
   - *Result: Your measurement lines will now correctly scale, shrink, and translate synchronously with the events on the timeline!*
3. **Tooltip CSS Restoration:**
   - The previously missing `.measure-tooltip-card` class has been formally added to `css/main.css`. Your data cards will now render beautifully with glassmorphism backgrounds and appropriate typography.

## Validation
* Toggle the Measure Tool and draw a line.
* Verify the HTML tooltip card renders at the end of the line.
* **Pan** the timeline left and right: The line and the tooltip should move precisely with the rest of the world data.
* **Zoom** the timeline in and out: The width of the line should expand and contract proportionally to the visible time span.
